How Bayn works — and how you get to trading live.

Bayn is a marketplace and a strategy lab. Quants build strategies and put them through a validation gauntlet; traders follow only the ones that survived and act on the signals in their own account. Bayn never trades for you — every order is yours to place and confirm.

The model

Two sides of one account

Your account can hold both. Trader is for following; Studio is for building.

Trader

Follow strategies that survived.

  • Browse a catalog of verified strategies across stocks, crypto, options, futures.
  • Follow one and receive signals — each with an entry, stop, target and plain-English reasoning.
  • Place the trade in your own broker, at your size, on your terms.
  • Log your fill and Bayn tracks your real performance vs the published record.
Studio

Build your own edge.

  • Compose strategies in a node builder or describe them to the AI Copilot (plain English → real graph).
  • Backtest on real history, stress with Monte Carlo and walk-forward.
  • Forward-test on live data as a paper session, then run it live.
  • Optionally submit to the Bayn catalog and earn a share when traders follow it.
The filter

Every catalog strategy clears five stages

This pipeline is the product. Only a small fraction of submissions clear every stage; survivors are monitored and pulled when their edge decays.

Your path to live · option A

Follow a strategy & trade its signals

The fastest route. Everything here works today except automated broker routing — which means you place the order yourself.

Create an account & onboard Live

Sign in, then set your markets, watchlist, risk-per-trade and account size. These personalize every signal's suggested size.

Follow a verified strategy Live Billing preview

Browse the Catalog and follow one. Free verified strategies activate instantly; paid ones show a subscribe flow — but billing isn't live yet, so no card is charged.

Receive live signals Live

When a followed strategy's conditions hit on live data, a signal fires with entry, stop, target and reasoning — on your Home and Signals pages.

Size it from the order ticket Live

Open the signal. The order ticket computes a suggested position size from your real account size and risk pref (set account size in Settings/Customize first).

Place the order in your broker Auto-routing preview

Today this is manual — you place the trade in your own brokerage. For crypto, Prepare Coinbase order copies a ready-to-paste ticket. Automated one-click routing to a connected broker is coming, not built yet.

Log the fill Live

Use Mark as taken with your real fill price. It's recorded to your Performance — real win rate, R-multiples and drawdown, and it's exportable to CSV.

Fastest way to be trading live today

Onboard → follow a free verified strategy → when a signal fires, place it in your own broker → log the fill. That path is real and end-to-end right now. The only manual part is the order itself.

Run it against real market data. Review the equity curve, and the Monte Carlo panel (bootstrapped from your actual trades, with an honest risk-of-ruin).

Run a walk-forward analysis — rolling train/test windows. Out-of-sample performance is the honest measure of whether the edge survives.

Forward-test on live data Live

The one gap between "signals" and "hands-off live"

Everything up to the order is real — validation, signals, sizing — and everything after the order is real — fill logging, performance. The single missing link is automated order routing to a connected brokerage. Until that ships, "live" means you (or your own agent) place the order; Bayn does the analysis and keeps the record. That's a deliberate design choice: Bayn never has custody of your funds or your broker.
